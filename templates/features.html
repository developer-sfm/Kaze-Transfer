How To Use Kaze Transfer

How To Use?

Kaze transfer currently needs a PC(server) to run. Once the sever is started, open any browser on your phone, other PC or any other device and type "http://[YOUR IP ADDRESS]:5000" in the search bar.
Once opened, the browser will ask for your permission to use the camera and capture and processs your video. After allowing your program will start and you can continue transferring images.

Features:

Kaze Transfer lets the user(or multiple users) to capture and share screenshots through gestures.
The user can only capture the screenshot on the server side through an "Closed Hand" gesture and the screenshot will be transferred to the client(Website) as soon as the user showcases an "Open Hand" gesture.

The client can also download the screenshot.

IMPORTANT!

NOTE: THE SCREENSHOTS AND VIDEO CAPTURED BY BOTH YOUR SERVER AND CLIENT SIDES ARE END TO END ENCRYPTED, MEANING NO OTHER PERSON(not even Kaze Transfer team) CAN ACCESS OR LOOK AT THEM.
